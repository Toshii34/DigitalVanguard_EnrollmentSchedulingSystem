ABOUT US

Alexis G. Santos National High School is a premier public educational institution in Bustos, Bulacan, committed to fostering academic excellence, moral integrity, and holistic student development. Guided by the standards of the Department of Education, our school provides a dynamic and inclusive learning environment that empowers young learners to maximize their potential. Through the collaborative efforts of our dedicated faculty and supportive community, we cultivate future-ready citizens equipped to lead and contribute meaningfully to society.

VISION

To dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation.

MISSION

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education where students learn in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment.

JUNIOR HIGH SCHOOL OFFERINGS

Alexis G. Santos National High School proudly offers the Special Program in Science, Technology, and Engineering (STE) for academically gifted Junior High School students.

🔬 Special Program in Science, Technology, and Engineering (STE)

The STE Program is a DepEd-initiated curriculum designed for students with high aptitude and interest in science, mathematics, and technology. It provides advanced learning experiences to prepare students for higher education and careers in the fields of science and engineering.

Program Highlights:

  • Advanced and enriched science and mathematics curriculum
  • Research-based learning and investigatory projects
  • Exposure to robotics, ICT, and laboratory experiments
  • Competitions, science fairs, and academic olympiads
  • Preparation for Senior High School STEM strand and college science courses

Note: Admission to the STE Program is based on academic performance, an entrance examination, and interview. Inquire at the school's registrar for details.
